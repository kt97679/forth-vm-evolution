# The fixed data layout, step 1 - measured (Iteration 734; engine/data-layout.ld, results/drafts/early-games-713.md)

A scratch clone of 576a30f4 (Iteration 733, numfirst applied) against the same tree without the layout. The engine's
small variables in one page-aligned block (.bss.hot), the tables a run touches after it (.bss.tab, ending with t_obuf
and the loader's heads - buffers used from their start), the other buffers of 4 KB and more after them (.bss.big).
GNU ld and lld both take the script (-T with INSERT); lab/evolve/data-layout-check.py lists each section's members
from a link map (run-tests: --self-test, two roles).

The record's memory, the largest median, the kernel workload's: 108 KB either way. Its medians of 7 read 104 or 108 on
the old layout from run to run (26 or 27 at the kernel workload) - the table's 104 -> 108 is that. Its small variables
had fallen on two pages already touched (with dispatch.1, heads and t_obuf - results/drafts/bss-pages-713.py): the
layout luck that went its way. Nine roles gain 8-36 KB at 8-byte cells, the three at 4-byte cells 12-24 KB; start-up
2-8 faults fewer; no engine byte, output or instruction count moves.

## Two variants measured and not taken

- **The small block straight after .data, in the file page's tail** (no page alignment): the nine gained as much or
  a page more (smallest 100 KB, fastest-x86-32 252), but least-memory paid a fault on every workload (BYE 15 -> 16,
  kernel 27 -> 28 over 25 runs; the same pages present, under gdb at exit_group): that page is the file's, mapped
  from the page cache, and the first write to it is a copy-on-write fault of its own - free only where .data is
  written anyway. Page-aligned (as taken), least-memory 15 and 27 again.
- **The four initialized variables a run writes made zero at load** (t_otty, t_seek0, jit_used, the JIT's l_tor -
  each a lazy "not asked yet" flag; 0 for it, the value + 1 or + 2 after): so the file's last page is never written.
  No fault fewer that the medians show, and the engines +2 to +48 B (portable-arm 48, best-all-round 30): reverted.
