## Iteration 401: seed 59, and musttail on every clang tail engine

**Seed 59** (its chain archive, forth-vm-evolution-chain-31b591c59-fury-20261019-032025): 24,802 measured at 32 bits,
2,383 failed - Iteration 400's prediction held. "died: corpus", 2,057: every one bfind expressed without tos, none of
the measured. "engine did not compile", 326: every one tail, none of the measured - GCC 168, clang with preserve_none
72, clang without it 86 (results/drafts/fail32-tailsplit-401.py).

**The 160 clang tail designs without musttail** (seed 58's 74, seed 59's 86). The owner asked for a view; decided:
musttail on every clang tail engine, at both widths (results/applied-after-seed62/musttail-after-seed62.patch, gen-tail.py
--musttail). musttail is not a trade-off but the guarantee the mechanism needs - a tail engine is right only if every
dispatch is a jump, and without it that rested on clang's optimizer and the ABI: at 8-byte cells the five arguments
travel in registers and the jumps came, at 4-byte cells they travel on the stack and none did. The trade-off in tailpn
is preserve_none, the register convention, and it stays a gene. Unlike the JIT, musttail exists on i386, so the 32-bit
engine need not differ from the 64-bit one; and the slowest at 32 bits would have selected against an accident of
code generation, not a property of the design. GCC's tail engines keep their optimizer, which makes the jumps at both
widths (GCC's musttail needs GCC 15).

With patches 1-6 applied, seed 59's clang tail designs a973c305bc and 0136e7294a and its GCC one f510231b27 live at
32 bits, as do engines-check's four tail variants, clang's without preserve_none among them. batch-check, the six: PASS - 6 patches on a fresh clone of de0b64f: run-tests PASS (engines-check 79 paths), validate 7 of 7 IDENTICAL, jail PASS.

So far no 32-bit failure in seeds 58 and 59 was a design that cannot exist there: each was a bug or a missing
guarantee. The mechanisms that cannot - the x86-64 JIT, scale 3 at 4-byte cells - are left out of the 32-bit build.

## At 64 bits (Iteration 402)

Seed 59's database at its home width: 27,185 lived, 8,489 screened (predicted off the front and not measured - Iteration
200; not deaths), and 188 died, all one class: "reach limit at scale 0 - not run". reach_lethal (evolve.py): a CV8 design
at scale 0 without both varcall and doesfar has calls that cannot reach across its image - run, it executes whatever
lies past their reach (Iteration 10: a fork bomb) - so it is not run. 74.5%% of the 188 lack doesfar (1.4%% of those that
lived), 41%% varcall (none of them) (results/drafts/fail64-reach-402.py). The designs' own, on every machine: an encoding
that cannot address the program it carries - there dead is right.
