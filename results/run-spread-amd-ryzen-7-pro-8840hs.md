**Why the best run fails: a rare lucky run.** With randomisation on, about
one run in 40 lands far from the rest - 8dd8a7a146 14.9 ms against a median

strays 15% under its median, and s6 never does. The best of N reports that
draw when it happens and not when it does not. The median of the same runs,
randomisation on or off, gives the same ratio (8dd8a7a146: 0.658 and 0.662
on cpu 2).

Decided: the evolver measures by the median of the rounds from Iteration 25
on (`measure()`; compare-fronts.py, image-ab.py likewise). Every speed
recorded before is a best of N - seeds 1-4 and the replicate - and is not
comparable with a median; the fronts are measured again by median next.

**Found on the way**: a placement exists where fib runs 34-38% faster for
some designs. If its cause can be found - what the lucky runs' addresses
share - every run could have it (GOALS.md).
